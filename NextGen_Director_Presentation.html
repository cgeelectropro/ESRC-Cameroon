01 / Overview

Executive Summary

NextGen is a fully operational, full-stack digital platform that unites education, entrepreneurship, and research in a single bilingual ecosystem built for Cameroon and the African continent.

What makes NextGen unique: It is not just a website — it is a complete enterprise-grade platform combining an LMS (Learning Management System), AI tools, payment processing, advisory services, community forum, research hub, and a real-time administration panel, all delivered in both English and French.

The platform is live and fully deployed at www.nextgen-en.com. The backend runs on Railway (NestJS, PostgreSQL, Redis) and the frontend is hosted on Vercel with automatic HTTPS and global CDN. The platform is legally registered as a Cameroonian enterprise and CFCE-certified.

02 / Vision

Platform Vision & Mission

NextGen was built to democratize access to quality education and entrepreneurial resources for Cameroon and sub-Saharan Africa.

📚
Learning Platform
A full LMS with structured courses, video lessons, quizzes, progress tracking, and verified certificates — accessible to any learner in Africa.
🔬
Research Hub
A curated repository of policy briefs, working papers, journals, reports, and datasets produced by ESRC Cameroon and affiliated researchers.
💡
Entrepreneur Ecosystem
AI-powered business tools, advisory sessions with mentors, funding opportunity discovery, and peer networking for African entrepreneurs.
Mission: To empower Cameroonian youth, professionals, and entrepreneurs through accessible knowledge, mentorship, and digital tools — contributing to sustainable economic and social development.

Target Audience: Students, working professionals, aspiring entrepreneurs, academic researchers, NGO partners, and development organizations across Cameroon and francophone Africa.

Flutterwave (recommended) supports 30+ payment methods including Visa, Mastercard, MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, and bank transfers via a single hosted checkout — ideal for African users.

MTN MoMo & Orange Money support direct payment with phone number — no bank card required, perfect for Cameroon.

Stripe accepts international card payments (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) for users outside Africa.

PayPal enables international transfers for global learners and diaspora users.

Security: All payment flows use webhook verification (Flutterwave verif-hash, Stripe signature). Payments are never processed client-side. The backend creates a pending payment record, verifies the transaction with the payment provider, and only enrolls the user after confirmed payment.

11 / Security & Compliance

Security & Legal Compliance

NextGen is built on enterprise security practices and is legally registered as a Cameroonian enterprise.

🔐 Authentication & Authorization

  • JWT access tokens (15-minute expiry)
  • Refresh tokens (7-day expiry)
  • Auto-refresh on 401 responses
  • Google OAuth login supported
  • Secure token storage (localStorage sync)
  • Cross-tab authentication sync
  • Role-based route guards frontend + backend

🛡️ Data & Infrastructure Security

  • HTTPS enforced on all traffic (TLS)
  • Vercel global CDN with DDoS protection
  • Railway isolated container environments
  • Database soft deletes (no hard data loss)
  • Webhook signature verification for payments
  • SSL encrypted database connections
  • Environment variable separation (never in code)
Legal Registration: NextGen / ESRC Cameroon is an officially registered enterprise in Cameroon, certified by the Centre de Formalités de Création d'Entreprises (CFCE) of Yaoundé. A LegalBadge with the official registration details is displayed on the About page and website footer.

The platform also includes dedicated Privacy Policy and Terms of Service pages, and complies with standard data protection practices — including not storing payment card data (delegated entirely to Flutterwave, Stripe, and PayPal's PCI-compliant infrastructure).
